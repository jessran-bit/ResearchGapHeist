# The Research Gap Heist

A rival team asked a chatbot for sources on **gender bias in AI algorithms**. The chatbot gave them 15 evidence cards. Some are real. Some are fake. Some are real papers with the wrong finding.

Your team's job: find the fakes, map the real evidence, and crack the research gap before the other teams do.

A referee bot checks every move and replies within about a minute. Scores stay secret until the end.

**What you need:** a GitHub account, a laptop, and your team.

---

## Step 0: Join a team

Each person does this once.

**[Join a team](../../issues/new?template=0-join.yml)**

Up to 5 people per team. Wait for the bot to reply "Welcome".

---

## Step 1: Check the cards

Open **[CARDS.md](CARDS.md)** and read all 15 cards.

For each card, find the actual paper. Use Google Scholar, the DOI, or the publisher's site. Then decide:

- **Real:** the paper exists and the summary is correct
- **Twisted:** the paper exists, but the summary is wrong
- **Fake:** the paper does not exist

Submit one form per card:

**[Check a card](../../issues/new?template=1-verdict.yml)**

Rules:
- Only your team's **first** verdict on each card counts. Agree as a team before you submit.
- Paste a link to the real paper as proof. If you cannot find it, write NOT FOUND and what you searched.
- Split the cards among your teammates to go faster.
- You can use any tool, including AI. But a chatbot saying "this paper is real" is not proof. Only the paper is proof.

Points: Real card right = 2. Twisted or Fake card right = 3. Calling a real paper fake = minus 1. No proof = half points.

---

## Step 2: Build your literature map

Group the cards you trust into themes. Group by shared idea, not by author or year.

**[Submit your map](../../issues/new?template=2-map.yml)**

The bot will draw your map. You can submit again to replace it.

Points: 1 for each real card on your map. You lose points for fake or twisted cards on your map, so only use cards you checked.

**Bonus (3 points):** Find two real cards that disagree or give different explanations for the same thing. A wrong guess costs 1 point.

---

## Step 3: Crack the gap

Look across your whole map. What is missing? That is your gap.

**[Submit your gap](../../issues/new?template=3-gap.yml)**

You will write:
1. The gap type (see the gap table from class)
2. One paragraph, at least 80 words, that connects the studies. Cite cards like this: (C01). End with "Therefore, this study aims to..."
3. One research question
4. A trace showing which cards each key word in your question came from
5. Your AI use log

If you use AI here, use it only to critique your draft, not to write it. Log what you asked.

---

## Step 4: Review a rival team

Pick another team's [GAP] issue from the [Issues](../../issues) list. Note its number.

**[Review a rival](../../issues/new?template=4-review.yml)**

Tick only the checklist items that are true, and give them one specific thing to fix.

Points: your team gets 2 points for each useful review (up to 2 reviews). The rival team gets 1 point for each box you tick.

Watch the **[LEADERBOARD](LEADERBOARD.md)**. It shows each team's progress but not their points.

When time is up, your instructor reveals the answers. The leaderboard then shows the final scores, and **ANSWERS.md** explains every card.

Good luck.
